About Rajasthan Travel Fair

RTF Concept — Connecting Travel, Culture & Commerce

Rajasthan Travel Fair

(RTF)

Rajasthan Travel Fair (RTF) is a curated 3-day B2B networking platform designed for the travel and tourism industry. It brings together Travel Agents, Hotels, and Destination Management Companies (DMCs) under one roof to connect, collaborate, and build meaningful business relationships.

The format encourages direct interaction — whether it's exchanging contacts, discussing potential partnerships, or understanding each other's offerings in a more personal setting.

The inaugural edition of RTF is currently in the planning phase and is scheduled to take place in 2026, with a vision to create a strong and sustainable tourism network for Rajasthan.

Rajasthan Travel Fair Familiarization Trip

(RTF FAM)

RTF FAM is an ongoing engagement initiative designed to provide travel agents with first-hand exposure to Rajasthan's diverse destinations. These curated trips focus on showcasing the region's rich history, vibrant culture, and most importantly, its wide range of hospitality offerings.

The first season of RTF FAM was successfully conducted from March 20th to 24th, 2026, and received an encouraging response from both participating travel partners and supporting hotel partners.

To learn more about the first season of RTF FAM, please click here.

Rajasthan Travel Fair Global FAM

(RTF GLOBAL FAM)

RTF Global FAM is the next step in our journey — an initiative aimed at bringing international travel partners to Rajasthan. It offers travel agents from across the globe an opportunity to experience the state's tourism potential, cultural richness, and hospitality standards first-hand.

The Global FAM is a 16-day immersive journey covering 14 key destinations across Rajasthan, offering a comprehensive view of the region's travel landscape.
